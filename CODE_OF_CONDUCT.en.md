# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our agreement

- Stay on the subject: discuss on the basis of evidence and settled decisions; do not guess motives or present suggestions as user approvals.
- Record honestly: mark unverified, blocked and failed items explicitly; never relax criteria to make results look better.
- Respect boundaries: do not silently rewrite sealed decisions or present process material as authoritative.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, reviews and document collaboration in this repository. Licensed under MIT; the maintainer will clarify how external participation works.
